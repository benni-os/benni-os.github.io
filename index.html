Company

Who we are.

We are Benni OS — an AI infrastructure company building the operating layer for the autonomous agent era.

We are based in Castanhal, Pará, Brazil. We build for the world. Our conviction is that frontier AI infrastructure does not require a Silicon Valley zip code — it requires precision engineering, public commits, and a system designed to survive production.

Every product we ship is auditable. Every spec has a decision log. Every commit is dated. We don't pitch vaporware. We push to main.

Our open-source work is on GitHub. Our platform runs at api.benni-os.net today.

  • Production FirstWe don't prototype in stealth. We ship in production from day one, with real uptime and real commit history.
  • Sovereign by DesignSelf-hosted. No cloud vendor lock-in. Data stays on our infrastructure. $0/month cloud cost is a design goal.
  • Open CoreThe building blocks are MIT-licensed. The platform is proprietary. We believe in showing our work.
  • Global from BrazilBuilt in Castanhal, Pará. Shipped to the world. Proof that tier-1 infrastructure can come from anywhere.
